## Privacy — návrh nové sekce (EN, jazyk webu)

### Ask DAJC AI

Ask DAJC AI answers questions about rules for road, heavy and oversize
transport in Europe using DAJC's verified data and official authority
websites. [Controller: as stated in "Site operator" above — to be confirmed.]

**Sign-in.** You sign in with a one-time link sent to your e-mail address.
The address is used only to send that link through our e-mail provider
(Resend). DAJC does not store the address itself: it stores a keyed one-way
fingerprint of it, which recognises the same user and enforces the daily
question limit. A strictly necessary session cookie (`dajc_ask_session`,
HttpOnly, valid for 7 days) keeps you signed in.

**Your questions.** A question is processed only to produce the answer and is
not stored by DAJC; neither is the answer. E-mail addresses and phone numbers
are removed automatically before processing. Please do not include other
personal data (names, registration plates, locations of a specific vehicle).
To produce the answer, the question is sent to OpenAI (understanding the
question and drafting the answer from the sources, with storage at the
provider disabled) and the drafted statements to Anthropic (independent
check). Short search keywords derived from the question — never the question
itself — are sent to Perplexity to find official sources. These providers act
as [processors] under their data-processing terms; [transfer mechanism to the
USA]. [Providers' retention, e.g. up to 30 days for abuse monitoring — to be
confirmed in the DPAs.] API data are not used to train their models
[to be confirmed].

**What DAJC keeps.**

| Data | Purpose | Retention |

| One-time sign-in token (hash) | sign-in | 15 minutes |

**Your rights.** You may request access to or erasure of the data linked to
your e-mail fingerprint by writing to team@dajc.eu from that address; since

## Terms — návrh nové sekce (EN, jazyk webu)

### Ask DAJC AI

1. **What it is.** Ask DAJC AI is an experimental, free information service.
   Answers are generated by AI from DAJC's verified data and official authority
   websites; every answer is labelled as AI-generated and shows its sources and
   the date they were checked.
2. **Information, not legal advice.** Answers describe rules in general. They
   are not legal advice, do not assess any individual case and do not say what
   a particular person may or must do or which penalty applies to them. Before
   any transport, check the current official source, your permit conditions
   and instructions from the competent authority.
3. **Answer levels.** VERIFIED: every statement is backed by a verified
   source. PARTIAL: every shown statement is backed, but the answer does not
   cover the whole question. DON'T KNOW: no answer could be verified; check
   with the competent authority.
4. **Limits and availability.** Up to [3] questions per verified e-mail
   address per day. The service has a monthly cost limit and may pause until
   the next month when it is reached. It may change, be suspended or end at
   any time. There is no entitlement to answers.
5. **Fair use.** Do not include personal data in questions, do not automate or
   resell access, and do not try to bypass limits or make the service produce
   content outside its purpose.
6. **Voluntary contributions** under section 3 give no benefits, no extra
   questions and no priority.
7. **Liability.** [To be drafted by the advocate, within mandatory consumer
   protection rules.]

## Česká verze pro posouzení (shrnutí obsahu)

**Privacy.** Přihlášení jednorázovým odkazem na e-mail (Resend). DAJC
neukládá e-mailovou adresu, jen jednosměrný otisk (HMAC) kvůli rozpoznání
uživatele a dennímu limitu; nezbytná session cookie na 7 dní. Dotaz ani
odpověď DAJC neukládá; e-maily a telefonní čísla se z dotazu automaticky
odstraní. Dotaz zpracuje OpenAI (porozumění a návrh odpovědi ze zdrojů, bez
ukládání u poskytovatele), tvrzení zkontroluje Anthropic; Perplexity dostane
jen krátká klíčová slova, nikdy samotný dotaz. Uchovávají se jen otisky,
denní počty (35 dní), záznamy proti zneužití (1 den), anonymní měření bez
vazby na uživatele a oficiální URL zdrojů bez uživatelských dat.

**Terms.** Experimentální bezplatná informační služba; odpovědi vytváří AI z
ověřených zdrojů s označením AI, zdrojem a datem ověření. Nejde o právní
poradu, neposuzuje konkrétní případy, nikdy „vy můžete / vám hrozí“. Úrovně
OVĚŘENO / ČÁSTEČNĚ / NEVÍM. Limit [3] dotazy denně, měsíční strop nákladů,
služba se může pozastavit. Dobrovolný příspěvek nezakládá žádnou výhodu ani
další dotazy. Odpovědnost doplní advokát.
