## Intro

Welcome to this week's DAJC European Oversize &amp; Special Transport Intelligence briefing. Our focus is on the latest developments affecting road freight transport with vehicles over 12 tonnes, prioritizing oversize, abnormal, and special transport updates first.

## Lead reports

### Cross-border special transport permits and procedures 2026 (Hungary)

Updated details on permits and procedures for cross-border special transport involving dimensions or weights exceeding the permissible limits.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Cross-border routes involving Hungary  
**Affected vehicles:** Oversize and special transport vehicles exceeding permissible size or weight limits  
**When:** from 2026-07-01

**Impact:** Transport with dimensions or weights exceeding limits now requires specific permits, including a Community license, CEMT permit, or bilateral authorization.

**Recommended action:** Ensure all technical transport data and planned routes are prepared for permit applications. Verify if additional authorization from CEMT or bilateral permits is needed for third-country routes.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated

*Source: [Schwertransport.de](https://www.schwertransport.de/en/grenzuberschreitender-sondertransport/)*

## Operator checklist

- Review cross-border permit requirements and check technical transport data before planning routes.
- Verify third-country authorization if applicable.
- Ensure compliance with updated tachograph requirements for vehicles between 2.5 and 3.5 tonnes when escorting.
