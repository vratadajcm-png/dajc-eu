## Intro

This week's DAJC Intelligence report focuses on critical developments for heavy, abnormal, oversized, and special road transport across Europe. Highlighted changes include new driving bans in Austria, Germany, and France due to public holidays and specific environmental impacts. Also featured are updates on extraordinary transport stoppages and escort requirements.

## Driving bans and exceptional-transport restrictions

### Exceptional-transport weekend movement ban (France)

Exceptional transport movement prohibited without departmental exemption.

**Where:** Nationwide French road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional transports without the necessary exemption  
**When:** Saturday 3 October 12:00 to Monday 5 October 06:00

**Impact:** Exceptional transports without a departmental exemption cannot operate anywhere in France during this window.

**Recommended action:** Confirm the departmental exemption before Saturday midday; differentiate from the general HGV weekend restrictions.

**Exemptions/conditions:** A departmental exemption is needed to operate; this is different from general HGV regulations.

*Source: [Bison Fute - Regime general des transports exceptionnels](https://www2.bison-fute.gouv.fr/regime-general%2C10852.html)*

### Germany-bound transit restriction on A12/A13 (Austria)

New HGV restriction towards Germany due to public holiday.

**Where:** A12 Inntal and A13 Brenner Autobahn  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods/articulated vehicles above 7.5t  
**When:** Saturday 3 October 2026 00:00-15:00

**Impact:** HGVs cannot use the defined corridor toward Germany unless exempt.

**Recommended action:** Avoid A12/A13 during the restriction; check route and permit conditions.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Applies on top of existing weekend bans; verify exemptions for specific conditions.

*Source: [RIS - Fahrverbotskalender 2026, BGBl. II Nr. 77/2026 § 1](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/ii/2026/77/P1/NOR40276924)*

### Day of German Unity public-holiday driving ban (Germany)

HGV Sunday ban applies on public holiday.

**Where:** Nationwide  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods vehicles over 7.5t and those with trailers  
**When:** Saturday 3 October 2026 00:00-22:00

**Impact:** Heavy goods vehicles cannot operate nationwide on this date.

**Recommended action:** Plan around the holiday restrictions or secure exemptions.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Standard §30(3) StVO exemptions apply; review the list for applicable transports.

*Source: [BALM - LKW-Fahrverbote](https://www.balm.bund.de/DE/Themen/RechtsentwicklungRechtsvorschriften/Rechtsvorschriften/Strassenverkehrsrecht/LKW-Fahrverbote/LKW-Fahrverbote.html)*

### Germany-bound transit ban for German Unity Day (Luxembourg)

Transit restrictions in place for public holiday.

**Where:** Luxembourg transit traffic heading to Germany  
**Affected vehicles:** Transit goods vehicles above 7.5t  
**When:** Friday 2 October 23:30 to Saturday 3 October 21:45

**Impact:** Germany-bound transit cannot proceed through Luxembourg during the ban.

**Recommended action:** Delay transit departures until after the ban period or secure exemptions.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Applies specifically for transit to Germany; does not affect traffic to other countries.

*Source: [Police Grand-Ducale - Driving bans on heavy goods vehicles](https://police.public.lu/en/legislation/code-de-la-route/routiers-poids-lourds1.html)*

### Public-holiday driving ban for St. Wenceslas Day (Czechia)

Sunday restriction extended to a public holiday.

**Where:** Motorways and Class I roads  
**Affected vehicles:** Vehicles above 7.5t and those towing a trailer  
**When:** Monday 28 September 2026 13:00-22:00

**Impact:** Restricted motorway and Class I road use during the holiday period.

**Recommended action:** Plan transit outside restriction hours or ensure exemption documentation.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Exemptions possible under statutory and Ministry of Transport conditions.

*Source: [Ministerstvo dopravy CR - Vyjimky ze zakazu jizdy](https://md.gov.cz/Dokumenty/Silnicni-doprava/Vyjimky-ze-zakazu-jizdy-%28povoleni%29)*

### Lueg/Brenner corridor special HGV restriction (Austria)

Special restriction on Brenner corridor imposed due to infrastructure works.

**Where:** A12 Inntal, A13 Brenner and A14 Rheintal/Walgau towards Brenner Nord  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods/articulated vehicles above 7.5t  
**When:** Friday 2 October and Saturday 3 October 2026 07:00-22:00/15:00
