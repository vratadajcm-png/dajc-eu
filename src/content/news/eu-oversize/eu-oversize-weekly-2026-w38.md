## Intro

This week's edition of the DAJC European Oversize Transport Intelligence report highlights critical developments across Europe impacting abnormal, oversize, and special road transport. Stay informed on new restrictions, permit requirements, and infrastructure updates relevant for planning and executing heavy transport operations.

## Driving bans and exceptional-transport restrictions

### Night motorway stoppages for extraordinary transport — A1 Bisko (Croatia)

Temporary suspensions near the Bisko junction for extraordinary transport continue through September.

**Where:** A1 Zagreb–Split–Dubrovnik near Lučko, Split and Bisko  
**Affected vehicles:** All traffic on affected motorway sections  
**When:** Through 30 September 2026, 22:00–05:00

**Impact:** Possible repeated short delays on A1 corridor, affecting HGV transit and oversize permit timing.

**Recommended action:** Check live HAK conditions before movement and build timing margin into planning.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Short operational closures, generally up to 15 minutes.

*Source: [Hrvatski autoklub (HAK) — Traffic Report, updated 8 September 2026 08:40](https://www.hak.hr/info/stanje-na-cestama/)*

### Exceptional-transport weekend movement ban (19-21 September) (France)

Exceptional transport movement prohibited without exemption from Saturday midday to Monday morning.

**Where:** Nationwide French road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional transports without departmental exemption  
**When:** 19 September 2026 12:00 to 21 September 2026 06:00

**Impact:** Exceptional transports without exemption cannot move in France during this window, affecting logistics planning.

**Recommended action:** Confirm departmental exemption before Saturday midday.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Departmental exemption allows movement during this window.

*Source: [Bison Fute - Regime general des transports exceptionnels](https://www2.bison-fute.gouv.fr/regime-general%2C10852.html)*

### Seasonal Class I-road restriction for wide special vehicles (Czechia)

Seasonal ban affects special vehicles wider than 600 mm on Class I roads outside built-up areas.

**Where:** Class I roads outside built-up areas  
**Affected vehicles:** Special vehicles over 600 mm wide  
**When:** 18-20 September 2026

**Impact:** These special vehicles cannot use affected roads during listed windows.

**Recommended action:** Verify if the vehicle is legally classified as special under Section 43(2) to adjust plans accordingly.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Separate from general HGV restriction in Section 43(1).

*Source: [Ministerstvo dopravy CR - stanovisko k Section 43(2)](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/Stanovisko-MD-k-omezeni-jizdy-kamionu)*

### Vereinfachte Bewilligung von Ausnahmetransporten (Switzerland)

Simplified approval process for exceptional transports and adjustments in driving bans, affecting night and holiday transport logistics.

**Where:** Switzerland  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional transports  
**When:** Effective since 1 July 2026

**Impact:** Facilitated approval for exceptional transports designed to streamline operations and aid logistics planning.

**Recommended action:** Utilize new simplified procedures for obtaining permits, especially for night and holiday transport needs.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Nighttime and holiday transport allowed for animals and perishable goods.

*Source: [ASTRA - Medienmitteilungen](https://www.astra.admin.ch/de/newnsb/rEO-ipY0tkkG)*

## Operator checklist

- Verify new temporary driving bans and adjust route planning accordingly.
- Engage with permit authorities to ensure compliance during extraordinary transits.
- Integrate toll system changes and potential costs into operational budgets.
- Recognize departmental exemptions for exceptional transport during restriction windows.
- Monitor infrastructure developments for potential logistical impacts and advantages.
- Utilize digital tools for live traffic updates and dynamic routing.
- Plan for infrastructure-related detours well in advance to avoid schedule disruptions.
