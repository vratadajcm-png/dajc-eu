## Intro

This week's briefing covers two truck toll developments that affect cost calculations for operators of vehicles over 12 t. Czechia has sent a proposal to raise toll rates from July 2027 into consultation. The Netherlands is applying a temporary reduction of its new truck charge until the end of 2026. No new binding oversize or abnormal-load permit, escort or route rule is confirmed for this week in the monitored sources.

## Lead reports

### Czech Ministry of Transport proposes higher truck toll rates from July 2027 (Czechia)

The Ministry of Transport has sent a draft amendment to the government regulation on toll rates for goods vehicles into inter-ministerial consultation. The new rates are planned to apply from July 2027. The aim is to bring Czech toll rates for heavy goods vehicles closer to those of neighbouring states. The ministry's announcement was published on 27 October 2026.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Czechia, tolled motorway and road network. The ministry cites the main transit motorways D1, D5 and D8 as a reason for the change.  
**Affected vehicles:** Vehicles over 3.5 t, which includes all vehicles over 12 t. Buses are excluded and their rates stay unchanged.  
**When:** Proposed application from July 2027. The draft is currently in inter-ministerial consultation.

**Impact:** If adopted, Czech tolls for heavy goods vehicles will rise from mid-2027. This affects cost calculations for transit and domestic work on D1, D5 and D8, including heavy-haul and project-cargo movements that use these corridors. The rise is not yet in force and the draft may change in consultation.

**Recommended action:** Do not change current Czech toll costing. Track the consultation outcome and the final regulation. Review quotations and contracts that run beyond June 2027 for toll adjustment clauses.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Buses are excluded. No exemption for abnormal or oversize transport is stated in the supplied evidence.

*Source: [Ministerstvo dopravy ČR (md.gov.cz)](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/Vice-prostredku-na-vystavbu-a-udrzbu-dalnic-Mytne)*

### Netherlands: truck charge temporarily cut by 22.3% until 31 December 2026

The Dutch government has temporarily lowered the rates of the truck charge (vrachtwagenheffing) by 22.3%. The reason given is sharply higher fuel prices caused by the conflict in the Middle East. The lower rates are in force now, so they apply during the target week.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Netherlands, the road network covered by the Dutch truck charge. Amounts payable are published by RDW.  
**Affected vehicles:** Businesses that must pay the truck charge. The page does not give a weight threshold in the supplied extract.  
**When:** 1 September 2026 to 31 December 2026 inclusive.

**Impact:** Charge costs for Dutch routes are lower until the end of 2026. Operators should use the reduced rates for current costing and invoicing, and plan for a return to the standard rates from 1 January 2027.

**Recommended action:** Check the current rates with RDW and apply the reduced rates to Dutch legs in quotations and invoices. Plan a rate review for 1 January 2027, when the reduction expires.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied evidence.

*Source: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/tijdelijk-lagere-vrachtwagenheffing/)*
