## Intro

The final weekend of August brings the last major wave of 2026 summer driving bans across Europe, stacking national weekend restrictions with country-specific exceptional-transport rules. Operators moving heavy or oversized loads anywhere on the continent this weekend - not just on any single corridor - should expect Saturday and Sunday to be effectively closed to standard heavy-vehicle movement in most of the countries below, with additional corridor-specific and exceptional-transport rules layered on top.

## Driving bans and exceptional-transport restrictions

### Summer Saturday and Sunday driving ban (29-30 August 2026) (Germany)

A summer-holiday Saturday driving ban applies on the BALM-listed motorway and federal-road sections, on top of the standing nationwide Sunday driving ban - the two must not be conflated into one all-roads rule.

**Where:** Saturday: only the officially BALM-listed motorway and federal-road sections (not a nationwide all-roads ban). Sunday: the general nationwide network.  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods vehicles (and combinations) over 7.5t gross vehicle weight; goods vehicles with trailers  
**When:** Saturday 29 August 2026 07:00-20:00 (listed sections only); Sunday 30 August 2026 00:00-22:00 (nationwide)

**Impact:** Heavy goods vehicles and goods-vehicle-with-trailer combinations cannot use the BALM-listed sections on Saturday, and cannot operate anywhere in Germany on Sunday.

**Recommended action:** Check the current BALM list of affected Saturday sections before routing through Germany on 29 August; treat Sunday 30 August as a full nationwide stop for the affected categories.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Standard exemptions apply (e.g. perishable/livestock transport, empty runs to the nearest suitable depot) - always check the current BALM exemption list for the specific transport.

*Source: [BALM - LKW-Fahrverbote](https://www.balm.bund.de/DE/Themen/RechtsentwicklungRechtsvorschriften/Rechtsvorschriften/Strassenverkehrsrecht/LKW-Fahrverbote/LKW-Fahrverbote.html)*

### Final summer-weekend HGV driving ban (28-30 August 2026) (Poland)

The last weekend of the 2026 Polish summer holiday driving-ban calendar restricts heavy vehicles and combinations above 12t.

**Where:** Nationwide Polish road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Vehicles and combinations of vehicles above 12 tonnes gross vehicle weight  
**When:** Friday 28 August 2026 18:00-22:00; Saturday 29 August 2026 08:00-14:00; Sunday 30 August 2026 08:00-22:00

**Impact:** Restricted vehicles cannot use the Polish road network during any of the three windows.

**Recommended action:** Plan Polish transit outside the three ban windows, and check the statutory exemption list if the transport may qualify.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Statutory exemptions exist (e.g. certain perishable/agricultural/international-transit categories) - check the current exemption list for the specific transport before assuming the ban applies without exception.

*Source: [WITD Radom - wakacyjne zakazy jazdy](https://www.gov.pl/web/witd-radom/wakacyjne-zakazy-jazdy-dla-pojazdow-ciezarowych)*

### Summer Friday/Saturday restriction plus the standard Sunday ban (28-30 August 2026) (Czechia)

A summer-specific Friday and Saturday restriction applies on motorways and Class I roads, in addition to - and with different hours than - the standard Sunday driving ban.

**Where:** Motorways and Class I roads  
**Affected vehicles:** Vehicles above 7.5t; vehicles above 3.5t towing a trailer  
**When:** Friday 28 August 2026 17:00-21:00 (summer restriction); Saturday 29 August 2026 07:00-13:00 (summer restriction); Sunday 30 August 2026 13:00-22:00 (standard Sunday ban)

**Impact:** Affected vehicles cannot use motorways and Class I roads during any of the three windows.

**Recommended action:** Plan Czech motorway/Class I transits outside all three windows; the Sunday hours are not the same as the Friday/Saturday summer-specific hours.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Individual exemptions ("povoleni") can be granted by the Ministry of Transport for specific transports - check before assuming the ban applies without exception.

*Source: [Ministerstvo dopravy CR - Vyjimky ze zakazu jizdy](https://md.gov.cz/Dokumenty/Silnicni-doprava/Vyjimky-ze-zakazu-jizdy-%28povoleni%29)*

### Section 39 seasonal weekend driving ban (29-30 August 2026) (Slovakia)

The Section 39 seasonal weekend driving ban applies on motorways, roads for motor vehicles, and Class I roads.

**Where:** Motorways, roads for motor vehicles, and Class I roads  
**Affected vehicles:** Vehicles above 7.5t; vehicles above 3.5t towing a trailer  
**When:** Saturday 29 August 2026 07:00-19:00; Sunday 30 August 2026 00:00-22:00

**Impact:** Affected vehicles cannot use motorways, roads for motor vehicles, or Class I roads during either window.

**Recommended action:** Plan Slovak transit outside the Saturday/Sunday windows; verify the current Section 39 exemption wording for the specific transport.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Section 39 sets out specific statutory exemptions - verify the current consolidated wording for the exact exemption list before relying on one.

*Source: [Slov-Lex - Zakon c. 8/2009 Z. z., Section 39](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2009/8/20260801.print.html)*

### Ministerial Decree 325/2025 weekend driving ban (29-30 August 2026) (Italy)

The 2026 driving-ban calendar set by Ministerial Decree 325/2025 restricts goods vehicles above 7.5t outside built-up areas, and - per the decree - extends to exceptional vehicles/transports even when authorised, unless a specific exemption applies.

**Where:** Outside built-up areas, nationwide  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods vehicles above 7.5t; per the decree, exceptional vehicles and exceptional transports even when authorised, unless a specific exemption or timing adjustment applies  
**When:** Saturday 29 August 2026 08:00-16:00; Sunday 30 August 2026 07:00-22:00

**Impact:** Affected vehicles - including, per the decree, authorised exceptional transports without a specific exemption - cannot operate outside built-up areas during either window.

**Recommended action:** Do not assume an existing exceptional-transport authorisation exempts the movement from this weekend ban; check the decree's specific exemptions for vehicles arriving from abroad or port traffic before departure.

**Exemptions/conditions:** The decree sets out specific exemptions and timing adjustments for vehicles arriving from abroad, port traffic, and other categories - always check the current decree text for the specific transport, including whether an existing exceptional-transport authorisation is still subject to the ban.

*Source: [Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti - Decreto n. 325/2025](https://www.mit.gov.it/normativa/decreto-ministeriale-n-325-del-12-dicembre-2025)*
*Also see: [MIT - Mezzi pesanti, calendario 2026 dei divieti di circolazione stradale](https://www.mit.gov.it/index.php/comunicazione/news/mezzi-pesanti-calendario-2026-dei-divieti-di-circolazione-stradale)*

### Exceptional-transport weekend movement ban (29 August 2026 12:00 to 31 August 2026 06:00) (France)

Exceptional transport (convoi exceptionnel) movement remains prohibited without the necessary departmental exemption, from Saturday midday to Monday morning (the window shifts around a public holiday - not applicable this week).

**Where:** Nationwide French road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional transports (convois exceptionnels) without the necessary departmental exemption  
**When:** Saturday 29 August 2026 12:00 to Monday 31 August 2026 06:00

**Impact:** Exceptional/oversized transports without a departmental exemption cannot move anywhere in France during this window - note it extends into the Monday of the following week.

**Recommended action:** Confirm the departmental exemption is in hand before Saturday midday; do not confuse this exceptional-transport rule with France's separate general HGV weekend restrictions.

**Exemptions/conditions:** A departmental exemption (autorisation prefectorale) can permit movement during the ban window for a specific transport - this rule applies only in the absence of such an exemption. Distinct from France's general HGV weekend restrictions, which follow a separate regime.

*Source: [Bison Fute - Regime general des transports exceptionnels](https://www2.bison-fute.gouv.fr/regime-general%2C10852.html)*

### Summer weekend driving ban for heavy vehicles (29-30 August 2026) (Hungary)

The summer weekend driving ban for heavy vehicles above 7.5t applies nationwide.

**Where:** Nationwide Hungarian road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Heavy vehicles above 7.5t  
**When:** Saturday 29 August 2026 from 15:00; Sunday 30 August 2026 until 22:00

**Impact:** Heavy vehicles above 7.5t cannot operate on the Hungarian road network during the ban window.

**Recommended action:** Check again shortly before departure for a possible temporary relaxation announced during an official heat alert.

**Exemptions/conditions:** The competent minister may temporarily relax the restriction during an official heat alert - operators must recheck shortly before departure, since a relaxation is announced close to the date rather than in advance.

*Source: [WKO Aussenwirtschaft - Ungarn LKW-Wochenendfahrverbot](https://www.wko.at/aussenwirtschaft/ungarn-lkw-wochenendfahrverbot)*

### Nationwide weekend driving ban (29-30 August 2026) (Austria)

The standing nationwide weekend driving ban applies, with thresholds that differ by vehicle type: goods vehicles with trailers above 3.5t, standalone goods vehicles and articulated combinations above 7.5t, and self-propelled working machines above 7.5t.

**Where:** Nationwide Austrian road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods vehicles with trailers above 3.5t; standalone goods vehicles above 7.5t; articulated combinations above 7.5t; self-propelled working machines above 7.5t - the threshold differs by vehicle type and must not be flattened to a single number  
**When:** Saturday 29 August 2026 from 15:00; Sunday 30 August 2026 until 22:00

**Impact:** Affected vehicle categories cannot operate on the Austrian road network during the ban window - a single flat weight threshold must not be applied across all vehicle types.

**Recommended action:** Confirm which threshold applies to the specific vehicle/combination type before departure, and check the standard exemption list.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Standard Austrian weekend-ban exemptions apply (e.g. combined transport, certain perishable goods) - check the current exemption list for the specific transport.

*Source: [WKO - Fahrverbote in Oesterreich](https://www.wko.at/transport/fahrverbote)*

### Additional summer corridor restrictions (29 August 2026) (Austria)

On top of the general nationwide weekend ban, additional corridor-specific restrictions apply on the A10 Tauern Autobahn (both directions), the A12/A13/A14 and Brenner corridor under the valid summer rules, and on B178, B320, B177, B179, B181 and B182 - including A10/Knoten Pongau exit restrictions where covered by a valid order.

**Where:** A10 Tauern Autobahn (both directions); A12/A13/A14 and the Brenner corridor under the valid summer rules; B178, B320, B177, B179, B181, B182; A10/Knoten Pongau exit restrictions  
**Affected vehicles:** Heavy goods vehicles and combinations, per the corridor-specific order  
**When:** Saturday 29 August 2026: A10 Tauern Autobahn 07:00-15:00; B178/B320/B177/B179/B181/B182 08:00-15:00

**Impact:** These corridor restrictions apply in addition to, not instead of, the general nationwide weekend ban - genuine destination/origin traffic in the corridor may be exempt.

**Recommended action:** Check destination/origin-traffic exemption status before routing through the Tauern, Brenner, or listed B-road corridors on 29 August, and recheck the A10/Knoten Pongau exit restriction.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Destination and origin-traffic exemptions apply - a transport genuinely starting or ending within the restricted corridor is treated differently from through-traffic. Check the current order for the exact exemption conditions.

*Source: [WKO - LKW-Fahrverbote Oesterreich Ueberblick](https://www.wko.at/transport/lkw-fahrverbote-oesterreich-ueberblick)*

### Sunday and night driving ban (30 August 2026) (Switzerland)

The standing nightly driving ban (22:00-05:00, every day) applies as usual, together with the Sunday driving ban.

**Where:** Nationwide Swiss road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Vehicles above 3.5t; articulated vehicles above 5t; vehicles towing a trailer above 3.5t  
**When:** Nightly 22:00-05:00 (every day); Sunday 30 August 2026 full-day ban

**Impact:** Affected vehicles cannot move at night on any day, or at all on Sunday, without a special permit.

**Recommended action:** Apply for a Sonderbewilligung in advance for any required Sunday or night movement, and confirm how it interacts with any exceptional-transport permit already held for the same movement.

**Exemptions/conditions:** A special permit ("Sonderbewilligung") can be requested for Sunday or night movements, and interacts with any separate exceptional-transport permit already held for the same movement - both must be checked.

*Source: [ASTRA - Sonntags- und Nachtfahrverbot](https://www.astra.admin.ch/de/sonntags-und-nachtfahrten)*
*Also see: [ASTRA - Sonderbewilligungen](https://www.astra.admin.ch/de/sonderbewilligungen)*
*Also see: [ASTRA - Orientierung fuer Gesuchsteller](https://www.astra.admin.ch/de/orientierung-gesuchsteller)*

## Operator checklist

- Check every condition in the individual exceptional-transport permit before departure - a permit can carry its own timing or route conditions on top of the general rules below.
- Verify national and regional exemptions for the specific vehicle category and cargo before assuming a ban applies without exception.
- Reserve suitable parking before the restrictions begin - do not plan to still be en route when a ban window starts.
- Recheck the Austria and Germany corridor-specific restrictions close to departure, since they apply in addition to (not instead of) the general nationwide bans.
- Check for last-minute heat-related changes, particularly the possible ministerial relaxation of the Hungarian weekend ban during a heat alert.
- Verify the live situation immediately before departure - national authorities can adjust restrictions on short notice.
