## Intro

For operators planning heavy or oversized transports in the upcoming week, significant infrastructure and route updates in Germany, Monaco, and Albania require attention.

## Main developments

### Germany

### Hildesheim BAB 7 road closure (Germany)

A lorry got stuck in a construction area on the southbound lane of BAB 7 near Hildesheim, causing road closure.

**Where:** BAB 7, Hildesheim, Germany

**Impact:** The incident may lead to significant delays or rerouting for heavy transport on this section of BAB 7.

**Recommended action:** Check for alternate routing around Hildesheim on BAB 7.

*Source: [Presseportal - Polizeipresse (Blaulicht)](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/57621/6337843)*

### Monaco

### Tunnel restrictions due to La Vuelta 2026 (Monaco)

Tunnel and road closures in Monaco due to La Vuelta 2026 cycling event.

**Where:** Monaco  
**When:** 2026-08-21 to 2026-08-23

**Impact:** Expect significant disruptions to traffic and potential delays for transport through Monaco during the event days.

**Recommended action:** Plan transport routes avoiding Monaco, or prepare for delays if transit is unavoidable.

*Source: [Gouvernement de Monaco - Actualites](https://www.gouv.mc/actualites/la-vuelta-2026-a-monaco-quels-amenagements-routiers)*

### Albania

### Rehabilitation works on Murrashi Bridge (Albania)

Rehabilitation works causing restrictions on Murrashi Bridge.

**Where:** Murrashi Bridge, Albania

**Impact:** The works may affect the load-bearing capacity and traffic flow on the bridge.

**Recommended action:** Verify the latest weight and access restrictions if planning to cross the Murrashi Bridge.

*Source: [ARRSH - Albanian Road Authority](https://www.arrsh.gov.al/specific-procurement-notice-rehabilitation-works-of-murrashi-bridge.html)*
