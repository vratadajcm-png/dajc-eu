## Intro

This edition covers the week of **9–15 November 2026**. In Germany, the A4 towards Olpe closes between Engelskirchen and Bielstein from 9 November while the Autobahn GmbH installs a barrier to keep trucks over 44 tonnes off the Wiehltal bridge. ECT in Rotterdam has again postponed enforcement of its mandatory truck-driver safety test, now to 5 January 2027. Further reports cover Italian abnormal-load permit lead times of about 90 days, tighter weight enforcement in Lithuania, exit waits at Serbian border crossings and exceptional transports announced on a main-road route in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

## Lead reports

### Germany: A4 closures at the Wiehltal bridge from 9 November while a barrier for trucks over 44 tonnes is installed

The Autobahn GmbH is installing a barrier system at the Wiehltal bridge on the A4 Cologne–Olpe near Gummersbach, North Rhine-Westphalia, according to its announcement as relayed by the Cologne Chamber of Industry and Commerce (IHK Köln) and the trade paper TRANSPORT (dpa, 23 October 2026). The barrier is to keep trucks heavier than **44 tonnes** off the 700-metre bridge, built in the 1970s. A weight restriction has applied since December 2024, a weigh-in-motion system was added in September, and traffic in the middle of the bridge runs in a single lane in each direction. ADAC Nordrhein (4 November 2026), citing the Autobahn GmbH, reports that checks still find trucks over 44 tonnes, exceeded axle loads and trucks not keeping the required 50-metre spacing on the bridge.

**Category:** Infrastructure restriction  
**Where:** A4 Cologne–Olpe, Wiehltal bridge near Gummersbach, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany  
**Affected vehicles:** All traffic on the closed sections; the barrier itself targets trucks over 44 tonnes  
**When:** Bielstein junction towards Olpe closed until 16 November, 05:00; A4 towards Olpe closed between Engelskirchen and Bielstein from 9 November, 05:00, to 30 November, 05:00; A4 towards Cologne fully closed between Reichshof/Bergneustadt and Bielstein from 23 November, 05:00, to 30 November, 05:00

**Impact:** For three weeks, traffic towards Olpe must leave the A4 at Engelskirchen and follow the detour signed with a red dot; from 23 November, traffic towards Cologne follows the U71 detour as well. The works depend on the weather, and the Autobahn GmbH says the schedule may change at short notice.

**Recommended action:** Plan A4 runs between Cologne and Olpe around the closures and add time for the signed detours. Do not route any vehicle or combination over 44 tonnes across the Wiehltal bridge, check axle loads as well as gross weight, and keep the 50-metre spacing on the bridge. Check the Autobahn GmbH's current notices before dispatch.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated.

*Sources: [IHK Köln](https://www.ihk.de/koeln/hauptnavigation/mobilitaet-und-logistik/sperrung-a4-7210464) and [TRANSPORT (dpa)](https://transport-online.de/news/autobahn-a4-schranke-sperrt-schwere-lkw-aus-194495.html), relaying the Autobahn GmbH announcement; [ADAC Nordrhein](https://www.adac.de/der-adac/regionalclubs/nrw/verkehr-sicherheit/a4-gesperrt-2026-sperrung/) — not the road authority's own publication; confirm current closures with the Autobahn GmbH*

### Netherlands: ECT postpones its mandatory truck-driver safety test again, to 5 January 2027

Trade outlet transport-online.nl reports (2 November 2026) that ECT has again postponed the introduction and enforcement of the mandatory safety test for truck drivers visiting the **ECT Delta** and **ECT Euromax** terminals on the Maasvlakte, Rotterdam. The new date is **Tuesday 5 January 2027**. According to the report, ECT took the decision at the market's request and after consulting the industry association, so that drivers have enough time to pass the test.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** ECT Delta and ECT Euromax container terminals, Maasvlakte, Rotterdam, Netherlands  
**Affected vehicles:** Trucks whose drivers deliver to or collect from these two terminals  
**When:** Enforcement from 5 January 2027

**Impact:** From 5 January 2027 ECT will actively check the test. Drivers without a valid, passed safety test will no longer be admitted to the ECT Delta and ECT Euromax terminals. Drivers who have already passed the test need not do anything for now; ECT will adjust the validity of passed tests to the new date and will explain how later.

**Recommended action:** Identify the drivers and subcontractors who serve ECT Delta or ECT Euromax without a passed test and book their test appointments now, so that terminal access is not refused from 5 January 2027.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated, other than the adjusted validity of tests already passed.

## Rest of Europe: sourced operational reports

### Italy: project-cargo shipper reports abnormal-load road permits now take about 90 days

Shipping Italy (4 November 2026) reports a talk at the third Break Bulk Italy conference in Venice by Pier Giorgio Pintus, logistics and export controls manager of the Swiss plant-engineering group Casale. He said waiting times for road permits for exceptional transports had risen from about 30 to about 90 days in five years, even with urgent procedures. For one 104-tonne component, the expected loading time via Koper was about three weeks shorter than via Marghera, so Casale shipped it from the Slovenian port; Marghera's share of Casale's shipments fell from 99% in 2025 to 48% in 2026.

**Where: Italy (Marghera/Venice port route cited) · When: reported 4 November 2026; permit lead times described as current**

**Operator action:** Allow for road-permit lead times of up to about three months when quoting Italian abnormal-load moves, and compare alternative ports where the Italian permit is on the critical path.

*Source: [Shipping Italy](https://www.shippingitaly.it/2026/11/04/pintus-casale-tre-settimane-di-differenza-nelle-autorizzazioni-ci-hanno-portato-a-imbarcare-fuori-dallitalia/) — Industry report — a shipper's account, not an official authority figure*

### Lithuania: tighter weight enforcement on the A1, A5 and A8 being prepared

MadeinVilnius (2 November 2026) reports that the Lithuanian Transport Safety Administration (LTSA) has signed a contract for six new mobile scales. According to the state road company Via Lietuva, a public procurement is under way for six high-precision automatic weighing systems intended to pass possible overweight violations directly to the register of administrative offences. Via Lietuva names the A5 Kaunas–Marijampolė–Suvalkai, the A1 Vilnius–Kaunas–Klaipėda and the A8 Panevėžys–Aristava–Sitkūnai among the first locations. From 2022 to 20 October 2026, LTSA officers inspected 2,412 heavy vehicles and found 1,386 violations of the permissible mass or axle load; 687 of them fell under the provision for driving without a permit with an axle load more than 4 tonnes or a mass more than 8 tonnes over the limit.

**Where: Lithuania, main transit roads including the A1, A5 and A8 · When: mobile scales contracted; automatic systems in procurement, no start date given**

**Operator action:** Check actual axle loads, not only gross weight, before Lithuanian transit runs, and make sure every load above the limits travels with a valid permit.

*Source: [MadeinVilnius](https://madeinvilnius.lt/en/transport/New-control-planned-on-Vilnius-Klaipeda-highway/) — Local news report — not an official authority notice*

### Serbia: trucks waited up to four hours at exit crossings on 3 November

Republika (3 November 2026), citing the Border Police Directorate's 05:00 figures and the Serbian automobile association AMSS, reports exit waits for trucks of four hours at Šid, three hours at Kelebija, Batrovci and Bezdan, and two hours at Horgoš. Passenger terminals had no longer waits.

**Where: Šid, Kelebija, Batrovci, Bezdan and Horgoš exit crossings, Serbia · When: 3 November 2026 (reported waiting times)**

**Operator action:** Build a waiting-time buffer into schedules for Serbian exit crossings and check live waiting times before dispatch.

*Source: [Republika](https://www.republika.rs/vesti/srbija/781434/amss-stanje-na-granicama-kamioni-cekanje) — Media report citing border police and AMSS figures — not an official authority notice*

### Bosnia and Herzegovina: exceptional transports announced on the Kamensko–Tomislavgrad route until 31 December

The Tomislavgrad news site RadioTG (28 October 2026) publishes a Herzegovina-Bosnia Canton notice informing drivers that exceptional transport (izvanredni prijevoz) will take place on main roads in the canton from 1 November to 31 December 2026, on the route M16 (Kamensko border crossing – Kazaginac), M16.5 (Kazaginac – Prisoje), M6.1 (Prisoje – Kolo) and M15 (Kolo – Tomislavgrad – connection to VP Baljci). The notice asks drivers for patience and to respect the traffic rules. It gives no dimensions, weights, escort arrangements or movement times.

**Where: M16, M16.5, M6.1 and M15, Kamensko border crossing – Tomislavgrad, Herzegovina-Bosnia Canton · When: 1 November–31 December 2026**

**Operator action:** If you plan to use this route in the period, ask the cantonal road authority for the movement schedule before dispatch. The notice itself sets no restriction for other traffic.

*Source: [RadioTG](https://radiotg.com/vijesti/hbz/obavijest-o-izvanrednom-prijevozu-2) — Local news report of a cantonal notice — not the authority's own publication; confirm with the competent authority*

## Operator checklist

- Germany: Plan A4 Cologne–Olpe runs around the Wiehltal bridge closures from 9 November and never route a vehicle over 44 tonnes across the bridge.
- Netherlands: Book the ECT safety test now for every driver serving ECT Delta or ECT Euromax without a passed test; access is refused without it from 5 January 2027.
- Italy: Allow up to about three months for abnormal-load road permits when quoting Italian moves.
- Lithuania: Check axle loads as well as gross weight before transit runs on the A1, A5 and A8.
- Serbia: Add a waiting-time buffer for exit crossings and check live waiting times before dispatch.
- Bosnia and Herzegovina: Ask the cantonal road authority for the exceptional-transport schedule before using the Kamensko–Tomislavgrad route until 31 December.
- Recheck the road authority's and terminal's current notices before travel.
