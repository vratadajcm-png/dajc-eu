## Intro

Stay informed with crucial updates affecting heavy, abnormal, and oversized transport across Europe for the week of September 21-27, 2026. Ensure compliance with new regulations, take note of route-specific restrictions, and manage operations with confidence.

## Driving bans and exceptional-transport restrictions

### Exceptional-transport weekend movement ban (France)

Movement remains prohibited from Saturday midday to Monday morning for exceptional transports without departmental exemption.

**Where:** Nationwide French road network  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional transports (convois exceptionnels) without the necessary departmental exemption  
**When:** Saturday 26 September 2026 12:00 to Monday 28 September 2026 06:00

**Impact:** Exceptional/oversized transports without a departmental exemption cannot move during this window in France.

**Recommended action:** Confirm the departmental exemption is in hand before Saturday midday.

**Exemptions/conditions:** A departmental exemption (autorisation prefectorale) permits movement during the ban window.

*Source: [Bison Fute - Regime general des transports exceptionnels](https://www2.bison-fute.gouv.fr/regime-general%2C10852.html)*

### Extraordinary-transport stoppage — A3 Okučani (Croatia)

New mid-September window added for extraordinary transport on A3 at Okučani.

**Where:** A3 Bregana–Lipovac near Okučani  
**Affected vehicles:** All traffic on affected motorway sections subject to temporary suspensions  
**When:** 15–24 September 2026, 23:00–05:00

**Impact:** Short full traffic stop may delay HGV movements and affect convoy/permit timing on A3.

**Recommended action:** Build a timing margin into A3 movements and verify HAK immediately before departure.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Short operational closures up to 15 minutes, not blanket HGV ban.

*Source: [Hrvatski autoklub (HAK) — Traffic Report, updated 8 September 2026 08:40](https://www.hak.hr/info/stanje-na-cestama/)*

### Seasonal Class I-road restriction for wide special vehicles (Czechia)

Separate seasonal restriction on Class I roads for special vehicles over 600 mm wide.

**Where:** Class I roads outside built-up areas  
**Affected vehicles:** Special vehicles, animal-drawn vehicles and handcarts with total width above 600 mm  
**When:** Friday 25 September 2026 15:00-21:00; Saturday 26 September 2026 07:00-11:00; Sunday 27 September 2026 15:00-21:00

**Impact:** Vehicles cannot use affected roads during specified windows, unlike general HGV bans.

**Recommended action:** Confirm if the vehicle is a "special vehicle" and route outside Class I-road windows.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Separate from general HGV restriction in Section 43(1).

*Source: [Ministerstvo dopravy CR - stanovisko k Section 43(2)](https://md.gov.cz/Media/Media-a-tiskove-zpravy/Stanovisko-MD-k-omezeni-jizdy-kamionu)*
