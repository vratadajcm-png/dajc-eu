## Executive summary

This edition covers **21–27 September 2026** and contains only changes that could be verified as operationally relevant to heavy, abnormal or exceptional road transport. Routine national or Sunday **Driving/Truck Bans are intentionally excluded** because DAJC maintains them separately.


## Wider Central Europe

### Germany — A7 / AK Rendsburg traffic layout changes on 24 September

**What changed:** The A7 Rader Hochbrücke project moves into a new traffic phase at the Rendsburg interchange. On **24 September 2026**, the entry ramp from the A210 coming from Kiel to the A7 toward Flensburg is closed and traffic is routed via U9. From 24 September, a new two-lane traffic layout applies in each direction.

**Where:** A7 / A210, Autobahnkreuz Rendsburg, Germany  
**When:** 24 September 2026; the construction phase continues beyond the week  
**Why it matters for heavy/oversize:** Autobahn GmbH explicitly flags Großraum- und Schwertransport (GST) users and directs them to the current clearance information for the route.

**Operator action:** Re-check the approved GST route and current clear widths before dispatch; do not assume an earlier route assessment remains valid after the 24 September layout change.

*Source: [Die Autobahn GmbH des Bundes — A7 Rader Hochbrücke / AK Rendsburg](https://www.autobahn.de/betrieb-verkehr/verkehrsmeldung/a7-rader-hochbruecke-tages-u-nachtbaustellen-im-ak-rendsburg-zur-einrichtung-der-bauphase-23)*

### Croatia — A1 Bisko: repeated short traffic stops for exceptional transport

**What changed:** HAK reports repeated temporary traffic stoppages at the Bisko interchange because of exceptional-transport movements.

**Where:** A1 Zagreb–Split–Dubrovnik, Bisko interchange, both directions  
**When:** Through **30 September 2026**, during **22:00–05:00**  
**Operational effect:** Traffic can be stopped repeatedly for up to **15 minutes** while exceptional transport is handled.

**Operator action:** Build short holding time into night movements on the A1 and verify HAK immediately before departure.

*Source: [Hrvatski autoklub (HAK) — current road conditions](https://www.hak.hr/info/stanje-na-cestama?lang=hr)*

### Croatia — A3 Okučani: exceptional-transport traffic stops continue through 24 September

**What changed:** HAK reports temporary stoppages in the Okučani interchange zone for an exceptional transport leaving the motorway.

**Where:** A3 Bregana–Lipovac, Okučani interchange, both directions  
**When:** **15–24 September 2026**, during **23:00–05:00**  
**Operational effect:** Traffic may be stopped for up to **15 minutes** for the exceptional-transport manoeuvre.

**Operator action:** For movements through Okučani on 21–24 September, verify the current HAK status and allow a timing margin around the night window.

*Source: [Hrvatski autoklub (HAK) — current road conditions](https://www.hak.hr/info/stanje-na-cestama?lang=hr)*

## Western Europe and connected corridors

### France — RD1006 Maurienne: 48 t / 3 m limit starts 21 September

**What changed:** The exceptional-convoy traffic regime at Pont Ravoire changes on **21 September**. After the preceding closure phase, the route reopens only to vehicles within the temporary limits.

**Where:** RD1006, Pont Ravoire / Maurienne corridor, France  
**When:** **21 September–12 October 2026**  
**Limit:** **48 tonnes maximum and 3.0 m maximum width** for the affected traffic regime.

**Operator action:** Any convoy above 48 t or 3.0 m must not rely on this section; re-check the authorised route and arrange an alternative before dispatch.

*Source: [SFTRF — Exceptional convoys / Maurienne roadworks](https://www.sftrf.fr/en/convois-exceptionnels/)*

### Belgium — Antwerp R1 / Oosterweel: severe exceptional-transport constraints during the conversion week

**What changed:** The official exceptional-transport roadworks notice was updated on **15 September** for the R1 works around Antwerpen-Oost, Antwerpen-Noord and exit 2 Deurne. During the conversion period, lane availability and closures materially reduce usable width; local detours are not suitable for exceptional transport.

**Where:** R1 Antwerp Ring, including exit 2 Deurne and connections around Antwerpen-Oost / Antwerpen-Noord  
**When:** Relevant restrictions continue during **21–25 September 2026**  
**Width rule in the works zone:** Where two lanes remain available, transport wider than **6 m** is not allowed; where only one lane remains available, transport wider than **3 m** is not allowed.

**Operator action:** Avoid the works zone where possible. If passage is unavoidable, check the lane configuration for the exact movement time and request an alternative authorised route early; do not use local detours without explicit approval.

*Source: [Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer — Uitzonderlijk vervoer wegenwerken](https://wegenenverkeer.be/zakelijk/uitzonderlijk-vervoer/wegenwerken)*

### Belgium — Antwerp port N180: daytime width limited to 3.5 m from 21 September

**What changed:** A new works phase starts on the N180 Noorderlaan in the port of Antwerp.

**Where:** N180 Noorderlaan, between Muisbroeklaan and Ordamstraat, direction Antwerp  
**When:** From **21 September 2026** to approximately mid-December 2026  
**Width rule:** Between **05:00 and 23:00**, maximum permitted width through the works is **3.5 m**. Wider exceptional transport can pass only between **23:00 and 05:00**.

**Operator action:** Schedule >3.5 m movements inside the night window or obtain an alternative authorised route before dispatch.

*Source: [Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer — Uitzonderlijk vervoer wegenwerken](https://wegenenverkeer.be/zakelijk/uitzonderlijk-vervoer/wegenwerken)*

### Belgium — Antwerp port Scheldelaan / Kruisschansweg: route regime changes on 27 September

**What changed:** The current phase on Scheldelaan runs through **26 September**; from **27 September to 20 November**, works move to Kruisschansweg between Scheldelaan and Van Cauwelaertbrug. The authority states that freight traffic is not allowed there and the sluice passages cannot be used during that phase.

**Where:** Antwerp port, Scheldelaan / N101b Kruisschansweg  
**When:** Transition on **27 September 2026**  
**Current exceptional-transport constraint:** When traffic is carried 2×1 on one side of Scheldelaan, width is limited to **3.5 m between 05:00 and 23:00**; wider exceptional transport is allowed only **23:00–05:00**.

**Operator action:** Movements planned from 27 September must be re-routed and, where required, covered by a new route permit before entering the affected port corridor.

*Source: [Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer — Uitzonderlijk vervoer wegenwerken](https://wegenenverkeer.be/zakelijk/uitzonderlijk-vervoer/wegenwerken)*

### Belgium — Machelen A1/E19: difficult or impossible exceptional-transport passage until 26 September

**What changed:** The connecting loop from the A1/E19 to the R0 inner ring remains restricted by bridge and road-deck works through the end of the target week.

**Where:** A1/E19 → R0 inner ring, Machelen  
**When:** Through **26 September 2026**  
**Operational effect:** The official exceptional-transport notice says passage is difficult to impossible. The temporary second traffic route is not suitable for exceptional transport, and the A12/R0 route over the Vilvoorde viaduct is also not a general alternative for wide exceptional transport.

**Operator action:** Do not dispatch a wide movement on the assumption that the signed general-traffic diversion is usable. Re-check the authorised exceptional-transport route and request an alternative if necessary.

*Source: [Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer — Uitzonderlijk vervoer wegenwerken](https://wegenenverkeer.be/zakelijk/uitzonderlijk-vervoer/wegenwerken)*

### Belgium — N78/N79 Riemst: restricted space for large exceptional transports

**What changed:** The third and final works phase started on 14 September and remains active into early October. Traffic alternates through the work zone under temporary signals, but the authority warns that space for large transports remains limited.

**Where:** N78 × N79, Riemst  
**When:** **14 September–early October 2026**  
**Operational effect:** Restricted manoeuvring space for large exceptional transports through the work zone.

**Operator action:** Verify route feasibility against the actual combination dimensions before movement and arrange an alternative route if the permitted envelope cannot be maintained.

*Source: [Agentschap Wegen en Verkeer — Uitzonderlijk vervoer wegenwerken](https://wegenenverkeer.be/zakelijk/uitzonderlijk-vervoer/wegenwerken)*


## Dispatcher checklist

- Re-check A7 / AK Rendsburg GST route conditions for movements on or after 24 September.
- Allow short night holding time around Bisko and Okučani in Croatia and verify HAK immediately before movement.
- Do not route >48 t or >3.0 m convoys through the affected RD1006 Maurienne section from 21 September without an alternative authorisation.
- Re-check Antwerp R1 lane availability and do not assume local general-traffic detours are legal for exceptional transport.
- Schedule >3.5 m movements on Antwerp N180 for the 23:00–05:00 window or re-route.
- Re-plan Scheldelaan / Kruisschansweg movements before the 27 September phase change.
- Confirm a legal exceptional-transport alternative around Machelen A1/E19 before dispatch.
