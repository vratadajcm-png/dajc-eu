## Intro

This week's briefing covers two developments for operators of vehicles over 12 t. ROADPOL's Truck and Bus enforcement week runs 16–22 November 2026, with Italy among the participants. Austria's amended toll law, which includes a new truck toll tariff system and digitised goods-transport permits, has a start date of 1 December 2026 for the digital vignette. Check the specific rules for abnormal and exceptional transport with the competent permit authority.

## Lead reports

### Austria: new truck toll tariff system and digitised goods-transport permits in amended toll law

The Nationalrat passed an amendment to the Bundesstraßen-Mautgesetz. It introduces a new tariff system for the truck toll (LKW-Maut) and replaces sticker vignettes with the digital vignette from 1 December 2026. According to the parliamentary report, the digitisation of goods-transport permits (CEMT context) was adopted unanimously, as was a cost-covering lock (Schleuse) operation. The parliamentary text was published on 20 November 2025. The supplied excerpt does not give the exact start date of the new truck toll rates or the details of the permit digitisation.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Outlook:** takes effect 2026-12-01, after the week covered by this edition  
**Where:** Austria, motorway and expressway network (national toll law)  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods vehicles subject to the truck toll; the digital vignette itself applies to vignette-liable vehicles. Applicability to abnormal or special transport is not stated in the supplied source.  
**When:** Digital vignette replaces sticker vignettes from 2026-12-01

**Impact:** Toll cost calculations for Austrian routes will need updating once the new tariff tables apply. Permit handling for goods transport may change with digitisation. The effective dates of these two elements are not confirmed by the supplied text.

**Recommended action:** Check the official ASFINAG tariff publication for the exact start date and the rates applicable to your vehicle categories. Update quotations and toll cost models for Austria transits. Ask the issuing authority how permit digitisation affects any permits you hold.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Not stated in the supplied source.

*Source: [Parlament Österreich](https://www.parlament.gv.at/aktuelles/pk/jahr_2025/pk1046)*

### Italy: ROADPOL Truck and Bus enforcement week, 16–22 November 2026

Italian police take part in the Europe-wide Truck and Bus operation coordinated by ROADPOL. Checks focus on tachographs, driving and rest times, loads and vehicle condition for professional goods and passenger transport.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Italy; the operation is Europe-wide under ROADPOL coordination  
**Affected vehicles:** Professional goods and passenger transport, with particular attention to heavy vehicles  
**When:** Monday 16 November to Sunday 22 November 2026

**Impact:** Higher likelihood of roadside inspections of tachograph records, driving and rest times, load securing and technical condition during the week. Violations can lead to fines or delays.

**Recommended action:** Before dispatch, check tachograph data and rest-time compliance, load securing, axle and gross weights, and vehicle condition. Brief drivers on the inspection focus. Allow for possible inspection delays in schedules, especially for time-critical or special-transport movements.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied source.
