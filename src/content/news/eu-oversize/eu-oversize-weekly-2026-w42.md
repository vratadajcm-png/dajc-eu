The week of **12–18 October 2026** brings a full exceptional-transport closure on part of the Maurienne–Fréjus corridor, overlapping German motorway closures, Dutch night works and a new temporary length restriction near Stockholm. Several changes fall directly into the night windows commonly used by abnormal transports.

Ordinary-traffic diversions are not automatically suitable or authorised for an abnormal load. Before dispatch, reconcile the current permit, exact approved route, gross and axle weights, dimensions, bridge capacity, work-zone width, escort conditions and the latest road-authority information.

## France–Italy: affected Fréjus/Maurienne category closes 12–17 October

**Official notice checked:** 8 October 2026; the live SFTRF page does not display a publication date.  
**Effective:** 12–17 October 2026.

SFTRF states that the affected exceptional-transport category on the **RD1006 Pont Ravoire–Pontamafrey section is fully closed from 12 to 17 October**. Until 12 October the separate limit remains 48 t and 3.00 m. On the A43 Maurienne, convoys 3.50–5.00 m wide and/or 72–120 t still require managed passage, with escort requests submitted four clear days in advance. Italy-to-France movements wider than 5.00 m remain prohibited until 17 October.

**Operator action:** do not treat the end of the earlier Fréjus ramp phase as reopening of the RD1006 category. Revalidate every France–Italy alternative against the permit and structural limits.

*Source: [SFTRF — Exceptional convoys](https://www.sftrf.fr/convois-exceptionnels/).*

## Germany: A40, A3 and A516 closures overlap 16–18 October

**Announced:** 1 October 2026.  
**Effective:** A40 from 16 October 21:00 to 19 October 05:00; associated A3 and A516 restrictions apply during the same weekend.

The **A40 closes in both directions between Mülheim-Winkhausen and Mülheim-Heißen**. Autobahn GmbH also warns that the A3 between Breitscheid and Oberhausen-West will close in both directions and the A516 toward Oberhausen will be closed between the Oberhausen interchange and Oberhausen-Sterkrade.

**Operator action:** the signed long-distance diversions via A52/A524/A59 are for ordinary traffic. Abnormal transports need route-authority confirmation and, where necessary, an amended permit.

*Source: [Autobahn GmbH — A40 closure](https://www.autobahn.de/betrieb-verkehr/verkehrsmeldung/a40-vollsperrung-zwischen-muelheim-winkhausen-und-muelheim-heissen).*

## Germany: A93 Regensburg full closure is planned for 17–18 October

**Announced:** 24 August 2026; this is an older notice with an event inside the target week.  
**Effective:** Saturday evening 17 October to Sunday afternoon 18 October 2026; exact times remain provisional.

Autobahn GmbH plans a **full closure of the A93 at the Regensburg interchange in both directions** to install a temporary bridge. The same official page also lists closure of the Abensberg entry and exit toward Hof from 12 to 15 October.

**Operator action:** confirm the final timing before dispatch. The announced diversions via Regensburg-Universität and Sinzing are not automatically approved for GST movements.

*Source: [Autobahn GmbH — A3/A93 Regensburg](https://www.autobahn.de/betrieb-verkehr/verkehrsmeldung/a3-i-a93-grossraum-regensburg).*

## Germany: A7 Egestorf–Evendorf narrows from 12 October

**Announced:** 28 September 2026.  
**Effective:** from 12 October to approximately the end of December 2026.

On the A7 toward Hannover, one lane remains available through the works while the opposite carriageway carries two lanes toward Hamburg and one toward Hannover. The Egestorf ramps toward Hannover close from **12 to 29 October**. Autobahn GmbH specifically directs GST operators to check current passage widths.

**Operator action:** create a time-bounded route-risk event and obtain the current usable width from the GST information before movement.

*Source: [Autobahn GmbH — A7 Egestorf–Evendorf](https://www.autobahn.de/betrieb-verkehr/verkehrsmeldung/a7-rifa-hannover-deckschichterneuerung-zwischen-der-as-egestorf-41-und-der-as-evendorf-42-ab-0610-bis-vrs-ende-dezember-2026).*

## Germany: A565 Bonn closes on two nights

**Announced:** 7 October 2026.  
**Effective:** 12/13 and 15/16 October, each night 19:00–05:00.

The A565 toward Siegburg closes between the exit and entry at Bonn-Poppelsdorf. Ordinary traffic is led off and back onto the motorway.

**Operator action:** an abnormal load must not assume that the short interchange diversion is geometrically or legally usable.

*Source: [Autobahn GmbH — A565 Bonn-Poppelsdorf](https://www.autobahn.de/betrieb-verkehr/verkehrsmeldung/a565-naechtliche-sperrungen-zwischen-der-aus-und-der-auffahrt-bonn-poppelsdorf).*

## Germany: Brandenburg cannot process GST enquiries until Monday morning

**Notice observed:** 8 October 2026; the page does not show a publication date.  
**Effective:** 8 October 15:00 to 12 October 06:00.

Landesbetrieb Straßenwesen Brandenburg says that **enquiries cannot be processed for technical reasons** during this period. The notice does not prove that every electronic submission channel is unavailable, so this edition does not describe it as a complete submission shutdown.

**Operator action:** expect processing delay and verify the status of any time-critical enquiry after 06:00 on 12 October.

*Source: [Landesbetrieb Straßenwesen Brandenburg — GST](https://www.ls.brandenburg.de/ls/de/verwalten/grossraum-und-schwertransporte/).*

## Netherlands: N7 Hoge Euvelgunnerbrug closes on two nights

**Announced:** 6 October 2026 at 13:17.  
**Effective:** 12/13 and 13/14 October, each night 20:00–06:00.

The N7 bridge near Groningen closes in both directions for annual safety testing. Ordinary traffic is diverted via Europaweg.

**Operator action:** the closures overlap common exceptional-transport night windows. Check the authorised route and do not assume Europaweg is an approved replacement.

*Source: [Rijkswaterstaat — N7 Hoge Euvelgunnerbrug](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2026/10/n7-hoge-euvelgunnerbrug-2-nachten-dicht-voor-jaarlijkse-veiligheidstest-12-14-oktober-2026).*

## Netherlands: A2 Echt–Roosteren has an open-ended lane closure

**Announced:** 6 October 2026 at 10:17.  
**Effective:** since 6 October; no end date was published in the notice.

The right-hand lane toward Maastricht remains closed between Echt and Roosteren after roadwork equipment was found too close to the barrier. Two of three lanes remain available.

**Operator action:** wide transports need a fresh RDW/DWO and permit-route check close to departure. Suggested ordinary-traffic diversions through Belgium are not automatically available to exceptional transports.

*Source: [Rijkswaterstaat — A2 Echt–Roosteren](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2026/10/a2-van-eindhoven-naar-maastricht-rechterrijstrook-blijft-dicht-na-nachtwerkzaamheden).*

## Netherlands: the planned A6 Urk 35 t limit was cancelled

**Announced:** 2 October 2026 at 14:30.  
**Effective:** the proposed restriction from 5 October did not enter into force.

A new structural calculation found the viaduct sufficiently strong, so the announced **35 t limit was withdrawn**. However, narrowed lanes remain between Urk and Ketelbrug until 16 October at 19:30, and some ramps remain closed longer.

**Operator action:** remove the false 35 t rejection from planning data, but retain work-zone geometry and permit-route checks.

*Source: [Rijkswaterstaat — A6 Urk](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2026/10/a6-geen-gewichtsbeperking-bij-urk-tijdens-werkzaamheden).*

## Sweden: vehicles over 12 m are temporarily prohibited on Akallalänken

**Announced:** 2 October 2026; updated 5 October 2026.  
**Effective:** from 12 October until the first part of E4 Förbifart Stockholm opens later in 2026.

After Akallalänken moves to its new alignment, vehicles longer than **12 m** are temporarily prohibited between Norrviksvägen and the connection to Hjulstavägen/Hjulstastråket. Trafikverket directs them via Norrviksvägen.

**Operator action:** long combinations and abnormal transports in northwest Stockholm must validate the indicated alternative against their permit and geometry.

*Source: [Trafikverket — Akallalänken](https://www.trafikverket.se/vara-projekt/projekt-i-stockholms-lan/e4-forbifart-stockholm/nyheter-for-e4-forbifart-stockholm/2026-10/akallalanken-flyttas-till-sitt-nya-permanenta-lage/).*

## Switzerland: Luterbach ramps close on four nights

**Announced:** 6 October 2026.  
**Effective in this edition:** 12/13, 13/14 and 18/19 October, 20:00–05:00; a fourth closure follows on 19/20 October.

ASTRA will adjust traffic management on the A5 at the Luterbach interchange. The affected ramps are Solothurn–Zürich, Solothurn–Bern and, on 18/19 October, Zürich–Solothurn. Bad weather can move each closure by one night.

**Operator action:** recheck the actual work night immediately before movement and verify any diversion against dimensions, mass and permit conditions.

*Source: [Swiss Federal Roads Office — Luterbach](https://www.astra.admin.ch/de/a1-luterbach-haerkingen-6-streifen-ausbau-naechtliche-rampensperrungen-und-anpassung-der-verkehrsfuehrung-bei-der-verzweigung-luterbach).*

## Hungary: M1 westbound remains unavailable above 3.50 m near Hegyeshalom

**Announced:** 25 September 2026 by MKFE, relaying a Magyar Közút road-manager notice.  
**Effective:** 27 September to 2 November 2026.

Transports wider than **3.50 m** cannot use the westbound M1 between km 166 and km 171 toward Austria during the works. MKFE says organisers must select another border crossing.

**Operator action:** treat this as a route and border-selection constraint, then verify the current road-manager and permit position before dispatch. The source is an industry association reproducing the road-manager notice, not a direct Magyar Közút publication page.

*Source: [MKFE — Hegyeshalom oversize restriction](https://www.mkfe.hu/hu/mediamenu/hirek/belf%C3%B6ldi-h%C3%ADrek/15885-tulmeretes-szallitmanyok-korlatozas-hegyeshalom.html).*

## Rest of Europe

No additional item was added merely to reach a numerical target. Absence from this edition does not mean that a country or route is clear. Local works, bridge constraints, permit conditions, police/escort requirements, port restrictions and short-notice closures still require route-specific verification.

## What operators should watch next week

1. **Fréjus/Maurienne:** the RD1006 affected category moves into full closure on 12 October and remains closed through 17 October.
2. **Germany:** the 16–18 October weekend combines the A40/A3/A516 closures with the planned A93 Regensburg closure.
3. **Night movements:** N7 Groningen, A565 Bonn and Swiss Luterbach works overlap normal exceptional-transport operating windows.
4. **False and stale restrictions:** remove the cancelled A6 Urk 35 t limit, but keep the narrowed-lane risk.
5. **New geometry:** the A7 Egestorf works and Sweden's 12 m Akallalänken rule require current route-width and permit checks.
