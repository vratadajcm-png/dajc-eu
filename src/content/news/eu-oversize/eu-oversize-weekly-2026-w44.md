## Intro

Welcome to this week's edition of DAJC European Oversize &amp; Special Transport Intelligence, covering developments relevant to road freight transport with vehicles over 12 tonnes. This briefing prioritises updates affecting oversize, abnormal, and special transport operations.

## Lead reports

### Weight Restriction Implemented on N307 Markerwaarddijk (Netherlands)

A new weight restriction has been enforced on the N307, prohibiting trucks over 15 tonnes.

**Category:** Infrastructure restriction  
**Where:** N307 Markerwaarddijk  
**Affected vehicles:** Trucks over 12 tonnes

**Impact:** Trucks exceeding 15 tonnes are now prohibited from using this route, affecting logistics planning for heavy transport.

**Recommended action:** Operators should reroute vehicles exceeding 15 tonnes to alternative roads.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None specified

## Rest of Europe: verified operational roundup

### Upcoming Winter Equipment Requirements for Trucks (Norway)

New winter equipment rules to apply across various European countries.

**Where: Europe · When: From November 1, 2026**

**Operator action:** Prepare for winter conditions by equipping vehicles with appropriate tyres and chains as per the destination country's requirements.

*Source: [LoadPlan](https://loadplan.eu/blog/winter-rules-trucks-europe)*
