## Intro

This week's briefing leads with oversize and special transport. Italy's transport ministry has set stricter criteria for exceptional-transport derogations for battery storage (BESS) containers. Czechia has published a draft toll increase for goods vehicles. The Netherlands is still running its temporary HGV levy reduction. One roundup item covers carrier capacity for Serbian operators under the EU Entry/Exit System (EES). Details are limited to what the sources report, and trade-press items are marked as industry reports rather than official permit conditions.

## Lead reports

### Italy: stricter derogation criteria for exceptional transport of BESS containers (UN 3536) (Italy)

Industry outlet ecoblog.it (21 Oct 2026) reports that the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (MIT) sent a note dated 6 July 2026 to the main road-transport associations. The note sets out how derogations are issued for exceptional transport of battery energy storage system (BESS) containers, classified as dangerous goods under UN 3536. According to the report, it applies more restrictive criteria than previous practice. The report is an industry analysis and the full text of the note was not available. Exact thresholds are not given in the supplied material.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Italy - national, with derogations issued under the MIT procedure. No specific roads or regions are stated in the supplied source.  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional (abnormal) transport of BESS containers classified as UN 3536 dangerous goods.  
**When:** MIT note dated 6 July 2026; reported 21 Oct 2026.

**Impact:** The report says the tighter criteria may make derogations harder to obtain. That could delay or complicate BESS project deliveries and affect permit lead times, route feasibility and cost for abnormal-load hauliers serving Italian storage projects.

**Recommended action:** Obtain the MIT note from your Italian association or permitting agent. Check how the new criteria apply to each pending BESS derogation application, and allow extra lead time in project schedules. Do not assume that existing approvals or earlier practice still apply.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied source. Do not assume any exemption applies to UN 3536 BESS exceptional transport.

*Source: [ecoblog.it](https://www.ecoblog.it/2026/10/21/bess-trasporti-eccezionali-restrizioni/) — Industry report — regulatory/route conditions require confirmation with the competent authority*

### Czechia: draft increase of truck toll rates (maintenance +5.8%, air pollution +34.8%, CO2 +70.7%) (Czechia)

Ekonomický deník (23 Oct 2026) reports that the government has drafted an amendment to the toll regulation, published by the Czech Chamber of Commerce for consultation. Three of the four toll components would rise for goods vehicles: infrastructure maintenance and administration +5.8%, air pollution +34.8% and CO2 emissions +70.7%. The noise component stays unchanged. The government expects about CZK 4 billion in additional annual revenue and cites alignment with neighbouring countries, notably Austria. Carriers warn that costs will be passed on to customers.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Czechia - national toll network for goods vehicles.  
**Affected vehicles:** Goods vehicles subject to Czech distance-based tolling; emission-class-dependent components are affected.

**Impact:** This is a proposal, not yet in force, and no effective date is given in the supplied source. If adopted, it would raise per-kilometre toll costs on Czech routes, especially for vehicles in higher-emission categories. That matters for transit pricing and contract calculations.

**Recommended action:** Monitor the consultation outcome and the final regulation and effective date. Review long-term transport contracts for toll pass-through clauses. Model the cost of Czech routings under the proposed rates.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied source.

*Source: [Ekonomický deník](https://ekonomickydenik.cz/zvyseni-myta-dopravci-zakaznici/) — Industry report — not an official authority notice*

### Netherlands: HGV levy temporarily cut by 22.3% until 31 December 2026 (Netherlands)

The Dutch government has lowered the rates of the vrachtwagenheffing (truck levy) by 22.3% on a temporary basis, in response to sharply higher fuel prices. Lower rates apply from 1 September 2026 to 31 December 2026. Amounts payable can be checked with the RDW.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Netherlands - national truck levy system.  
**Affected vehicles:** Operators liable for the Dutch vrachtwagenheffing.  
**When:** 1 September 2026 to 31 December 2026.

**Impact:** Levy costs on Dutch routes are lower throughout the target week. The reduced rates end on 31 December 2026, so pricing for 2027 contracts should assume a return to the standard rates unless extended.

**Recommended action:** Check levy calculations against the reduced rates. Make sure customer quotes and surcharges reflect the temporary reduction and its end date. Verify amounts with the RDW.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied source.

*Source: [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetswijzigingen/tijdelijk-lagere-vrachtwagenheffing/)*

## Rest of Europe: sourced operational reports

### Serbia: EES 90/180-day limit removes carrier capacity and raises haulage prices (Serbia)

Business outlet Biznis i Finansije (19 Oct 2026) reports that the 90-days-in-180 stay limit for professional drivers from Serbia, applied via the EU Entry/Exit System (EES), takes about 100 Serbian trucks out of traffic every day. The outlet says the capacity shortage raises haulage prices by up to four times, delays deliveries to EU customers and hits exporters as well as carriers. It also cites a September IMF assessment of higher operating costs for transport and logistics firms across the Western Balkans. Rail diversion is being discussed but needs further investment. This is an industry report, not an official rule notice.

**Where: Serbia and Western Balkans road freight to and from the EU.**

**Operator action:** Track each driver's days in the Schengen area against the 90/180 limit. Secure capacity early for Serbia-EU lanes and reflect higher rates in quotes. Consider EU-based drivers or subcontractors where practical.

*Source: [Biznis i Finansije](https://bif.rs/2026/10/muke-sa-ees-om-klin-u-tockove/) — Industry report — not an official authority notice*
