## Intro

This week's briefing leads with oversize and abnormal transport. Italy's Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport has tightened the criteria for BESS container derogations, and the Netherlands now has an axle and weight limit on the N307 Houtribdijk. Further items cover a Czech draft toll increase and border waiting times at Batrovci–Bajakovo. Check the official source before dispatching any movement.

## Lead reports

### Italy: stricter criteria for exceptional-transport derogations for BESS containers (UN 3536) (Italy)

An industry report by ecoblog.it (21 Oct 2026) describes a note issued by the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (MIT) on 6 July 2026 to the main road haulage associations. The note clarifies the procedures for granting derogations for exceptional transport of BESS (battery energy storage system) containers classified as dangerous goods under UN 3536. It applies more restrictive criteria than before. The report warns that this could delay or block storage projects in Italy.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Italy, national level. The report gives no specific roads or regions.  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional transports of BESS containers classified as dangerous goods (UN 3536) that need a derogation.

**Impact:** Derogations for BESS container movements may be harder to obtain, which can lengthen lead times, narrow route and timing options and raise costs for project-cargo and energy logistics. This is an industry report on an MIT note. The exact criteria are not detailed in the supplied material.

**Recommended action:** Obtain the MIT note of 6 July 2026 and confirm the current derogation criteria with the competent permit authority before quoting or scheduling BESS moves. Allow extra time for permit applications and check that the vehicle, load and ADR documentation fit the stricter criteria.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied material. Do not assume any exemption applies.

*Source: [ecoblog.it](https://www.ecoblog.it/2026/10/21/bess-trasporti-eccezionali-restrizioni/) — Industry report — regulatory/route conditions require confirmation with the competent authority*

### Netherlands: 7.3 t axle-load and 15 t weight limit on N307 Houtribdijk in force since 21 October (Netherlands)

Rijkswaterstaat announced an axle-load limit of 7.3 t and a weight limit of 15 t on the Houtribdijk (N307, also known as the Markerwaarddijk), applying from 21 October 2026. Heavy traffic can no longer use this road between Lelystad and Enkhuizen in either direction. The measure is intended to prevent further damage to the Houtribbruggen and keep them available to other traffic. The notice was first published on 27 Aug 2026 and last updated on 28 Sep 2026.

**Category:** Infrastructure restriction  
**Where:** N307 Houtribdijk (Markerwaarddijk) between Lelystad and Enkhuizen, both directions.  
**Affected vehicles:** Heavy traffic, in particular lorries, above the 7.3 t axle-load and 15 t weight limits. Applicability to abnormal or exceptional transports is not stated in the supplied material.  
**When:** From 21 October 2026. No end date is given in the source.

**Impact:** Vehicles over 12 t, including loaded heavy-haul combinations, cannot use this crossing and must take another route between Lelystad and Enkhuizen. Check route plans and permit routes that include the N307.

**Recommended action:** Remove the N307 Houtribdijk from route plans and navigation profiles for heavy vehicles. For abnormal loads, check with the Dutch permit authority whether any permit route uses this dike road.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied material.

*Source: [Rijkswaterstaat](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2026/08/gewichtsbeperking-houtribdijk-n307)*

## Rest of Europe: sourced operational reports

### Serbia/Croatia: waits of 6–7 hours for trucks at Batrovci–Bajakovo (Serbia)

Logistics Market, citing Auto-moto savez Srbije (AMSS), reports that trucks leaving Serbia at Batrovci waited about six hours on 17 Oct 2026 and about seven hours on 18 Oct 2026. Congestion was also recorded on the Croatian side at Bajakovo. The report published on 19 Oct 2026 calls this a recurring pressure point on the corridor between Serbia and the EU.

**Where: Border crossing Batrovci (Serbia) – Bajakovo (Croatia). · When: 17–18 October 2026 (reported waiting times).**

**Operator action:** Build a waiting-time buffer into schedules on this crossing and check live waiting times before dispatch. Consider other crossings where timing is critical, and plan rest periods around possible queues.
