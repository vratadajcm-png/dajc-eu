## Intro

This edition covers the week of **26 October–1 November 2026**. Since 21 October, heavy traffic can no longer cross the Houtribsluizen bridges on the N307 Markerwaarddijk between Lelystad and Enkhuizen: Rijkswaterstaat limits them to 15 t gross weight and 7.3 t per axle, possibly for years. The detour for heavy traffic runs via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk.

## Lead reports

### Netherlands: 15-tonne gross-weight and 7.3-tonne axle-load limits on the N307 Markerwaarddijk

The Dutch road authority Rijkswaterstaat confirms that restrictions have applied since **21 October 2026** to the bridges over the Houtribsluizen near Lelystad on the N307 Markerwaarddijk (Houtribdijk). The limits are **15 tonnes for the total weight of a vehicle or vehicle combination, including its load**, and **7.3 tonnes on any single axle**. Both conditions must be met. These restrictions are especially important for heavy and exceptional-load route planning.

**Category:** Infrastructure restriction  
**Where:** N307 Markerwaarddijk / Houtribdijk, bridges over the Houtribsluizen near Lelystad, Netherlands  
**Effective:** 21 October 2026  
**Affected vehicles:** Any vehicle or combination exceeding 15 tonnes total actual weight, or exceeding 7.3 tonnes on an axle  
**Impact:** Heavy transport exceeding either limit can no longer use the N307 between Lelystad and Enkhuizen and must detour via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk. Rijkswaterstaat says the measure may remain in force for years because of structural problems with the bridges.

**Recommended action:** Dispatchers should check the actual gross combination weight and individual axle loads before assigning this corridor. Rijkswaterstaat's detour for heavy traffic runs **via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk**; signs on the surrounding A6 and A7 point to the restriction and the detours. Its detailed Houtribdijk restriction notice also states that existing exemptions conflicting with the new restriction cease to apply. Verify the entire alternative route and obtain any required revised abnormal-load permit before departure; a suggested detour does not amend an issued permit.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Passenger cars, emergency services, vans and other vehicles meeting the posted restrictions may continue to use the route. No general exemption for oversize transport is confirmed by the cited authority notice.

*Official sources: [Rijkswaterstaat — N307 weight restriction from 21 October 2026](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2026/09/n307-gewichtsbeperking-markerwaarddijk-vanaf-21-oktober-2026) and [detailed Houtribdijk weight and axle-load restrictions](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/water/waterbeheer/bescherming-tegen-het-water/waterkeringen/dijken/houtribdijk/aslast-en-gewichtsbeperking-houtribdijk).*

## Operator checklist

- Check the N307 Markerwaarddijk against **both** the 15-tonne gross combination limit and the 7.3-tonne axle-load limit.
- Plan the detour via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk and check any applicable abnormal-load permit before dispatch; never silently change a permitted route.
- Treat any existing exemption that conflicts with the new limits as invalid, as stated by Rijkswaterstaat; confirm revised permits with the competent authority.
- Recheck the road authority's current notice and roadside signs before travel.

*Correction (24 October 2026): an earlier version said that Rijkswaterstaat directs heavy traffic to the A6 and A7. Signs on those motorways point to the restriction; the detour itself runs via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk.*
