## Intro

One development qualifies for this edition. Sweden has put forward a consultation proposal to extend the road network open to 34.5 m, 74 t combinations. It is reported by trade and media sources, not as an adopted rule. Operators running long, heavy combinations in the Nordics should track it.

## Lead reports

### Sweden proposes extending the 34.5 m, 74 t combination network by about 3,800 km (Sweden)

An industry report published on 19 October 2026 says Sweden plans to add about 3,800 km of roads to the network open to 34.5 m, 74 t vehicle combinations. The report says the draft amendments to the road traffic rules have been prepared and put out for official consultation. It cites Trafikverket via 40ton.net and says the change is expected to take effect on 1 January 2027. It describes the move as following three years of operating double-trailer combinations. Before the change, the permitted network was about 6,800 km. It consisted mainly of main routes in southern Sweden and a key northern route towards the land border. The proposal is not yet in force. The exact road list should be checked against the official Trafikverket consultation material.

**Category:** Infrastructure restriction  
**Where:** Sweden, on the national road network designated for 34.5 m combinations. The extension covers about 3,800 km of additional roads. The specific road identifiers are not given in the supplied source.  
**Affected vehicles:** Vehicle combinations with two full-size trailers, up to 34.5 m in length and 74 t in weight. This is reported as a high-capacity transport regime, not an abnormal-load permit rule.  
**When:** Consultation under way as of 19 October 2026. The expected entry into force is 1 January 2027.

**Impact:** If adopted, the proposal would roughly increase the Swedish network available to 34.5 m, 74 t combinations by more than half. That would affect route planning, equipment choice and cost calculations for long-haul and Nordic transit operations. Until the rules are adopted, current route limits remain unchanged. This is an industry report from a secondary media outlet, not an officially validated legal condition.

**Recommended action:** Check the official Trafikverket consultation documents for the exact road list and conditions. Do not plan 34.5 m, 74 t operations on the new roads before the amendment is adopted. Consider whether to submit consultation input if your operations would be affected. Review fleet and trailer configuration for planned 2027 Swedish work.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the source. Applicability to abnormal or exceptional transport permits is not confirmed.

- Check the Trafikverket consultation material for the final road list before planning any 34.5 m, 74 t movements in Sweden.
- Treat the Swedish extension as a proposal until the amendment is formally adopted.
- Check the official source for the exact conditions before relying on a trade-press summary for permit or route decisions.
