## Intro

This edition covers the week of **26 October–1 November 2026**. Since 21 October, heavy traffic can no longer cross the Houtribsluizen bridges on the N307 Markerwaarddijk between Lelystad and Enkhuizen. Rijkswaterstaat limits them to 15 t gross weight and 7.3 t per axle, possibly for years; the detour for heavy traffic runs via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk. In Italy, a transport ministry note restricts derogations for abnormal-load transports of battery storage (BESS) containers. On the Serbia–EU corridor, trucks waited up to seven hours at Batrovci–Bajakovo last weekend.

## Lead reports

### Netherlands: 15-tonne gross-weight and 7.3-tonne axle-load limits on the N307 Markerwaarddijk

The Dutch road authority Rijkswaterstaat confirms that restrictions have applied since **21 October 2026** to the bridges over the Houtribsluizen near Lelystad on the N307 Markerwaarddijk (Houtribdijk). The limits are **15 tonnes for the total weight of a vehicle or vehicle combination, including its load**, and **7.3 tonnes on any single axle**. Both conditions must be met. These restrictions are especially important for heavy and exceptional-load route planning.

**Category:** Infrastructure restriction  
**Where:** N307 Markerwaarddijk / Houtribdijk, bridges over the Houtribsluizen near Lelystad, Netherlands  
**Effective:** 21 October 2026  
**Affected vehicles:** Any vehicle or combination exceeding 15 tonnes total actual weight, or exceeding 7.3 tonnes on an axle  
**Impact:** Heavy transport exceeding either limit can no longer use the N307 between Lelystad and Enkhuizen and must detour via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk. Rijkswaterstaat says the measure may remain in force for years because of structural problems with the bridges.

**Recommended action:** Dispatchers should check the actual gross combination weight and individual axle loads before assigning this corridor. Rijkswaterstaat's detour for heavy traffic runs **via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk**; signs on the surrounding A6 and A7 point to the restriction and the detours. Its detailed Houtribdijk restriction notice also states that existing exemptions conflicting with the new restriction cease to apply. Verify the entire alternative route and obtain any required revised abnormal-load permit before departure; a suggested detour does not amend an issued permit.

**Exemptions/conditions:** Passenger cars, emergency services, vans and other vehicles meeting the posted restrictions may continue to use the route. No general exemption for oversize transport is confirmed by the cited authority notice.

*Official sources: [Rijkswaterstaat — N307 weight restriction from 21 October 2026](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2026/09/n307-gewichtsbeperking-markerwaarddijk-vanaf-21-oktober-2026) and [detailed Houtribdijk weight and axle-load restrictions](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/water/waterbeheer/bescherming-tegen-het-water/waterkeringen/dijken/houtribdijk/aslast-en-gewichtsbeperking-houtribdijk).*

### Italy: derogations for abnormal-load transports of BESS containers (UN 3536) are restricted

According to an industry report by ecoblog.it (21 October 2026), the Directorate-General for Motorisation of the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (MIT) sent a note dated **6 July 2026** to the main road haulage associations. The note does not create a new rule. It sets out how to apply **Article 368 of the implementing regulation of the Italian Highway Code**, which prohibits carrying dangerous goods on exceptional vehicles unless a derogation is granted, and such derogations must be "individual, specific and justified". Large battery energy storage system (BESS) containers are classified as **UN 3536** dangerous goods and usually travel as abnormal loads, so they fall under this rule. The report says the note also **limits derogations for energy storage transports to strategic nodes of the network**, and that it applies stricter criteria than before.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Italy, national; the report does not name specific roads or regions  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional (abnormal-load) vehicles carrying BESS containers classified as UN 3536  
**Impact:** Derogations for BESS deliveries may be harder or slower to obtain, and sites outside the strategic nodes may not qualify at all. The report warns that this can hold up storage projects that are already authorised.

**Recommended action:** Obtain the MIT note of 6 July 2026 from your association or the permit authority. Check each pending BESS move against the "individual, specific and justified" test and the strategic-node criterion, document each derogation request in detail and allow extra permit lead time in project schedules.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the report. This is a trade-press account of the note, not the official text; confirm the criteria with the competent authority.

## Rest of Europe: sourced operational reports

### Serbia/Croatia: trucks waited six to seven hours at Batrovci–Bajakovo

Logistics Market (19 October 2026), citing the Serbian automobile association AMSS, reports that trucks leaving Serbia at Batrovci waited about six hours on 17 October and about seven hours on 18 October 2026. Congestion was also recorded on the Croatian side at Bajakovo. The crossing is on Corridor 10, the main road link between Serbia and Croatia, Slovenia and other EU markets, and the report describes the delays as a recurring pressure point.

**Where: Batrovci (Serbia) – Bajakovo (Croatia) border crossing · When: 17–18 October 2026 (reported waiting times)**

**Operator action:** Build a waiting-time buffer into schedules for this crossing, check live waiting times before dispatch and plan driver rest periods around possible queues.

*Source: [Logistics Market](https://logisticsmarket.rs/otezan-transport-robe-ka-evropskoj-uniji-batrovci-ponovo-pod-pritiskom/) — Industry report — not an official authority notice*

## Operator checklist

- Netherlands: Check the N307 Markerwaarddijk against **both** the 15-tonne gross combination limit and the 7.3-tonne axle-load limit.
- Netherlands: Plan the detour via Amsterdam or the Afsluitdijk and check any applicable abnormal-load permit before dispatch; never silently change a permitted route.
- Netherlands: Treat any existing exemption that conflicts with the new limits as invalid, as stated by Rijkswaterstaat; confirm revised permits with the competent authority.
- Italy: Check pending BESS container moves against the MIT note of 6 July 2026 and allow extra derogation lead time.
- Serbia/Croatia: Add a waiting-time buffer for Batrovci–Bajakovo and check live waiting times before dispatch.
- Recheck the road authority's current notice and roadside signs before travel.
