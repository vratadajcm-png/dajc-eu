## Intro

This week's briefing covers two verified developments. In Italy, the Ministry's guidance on derogations for exceptional transport of BESS containers (UN 3536) is now reported as tightening permit criteria. In Czechia, a government draft would raise HGV toll rates. Both are reported with the source's provenance and with the limits of the available evidence made explicit.

## Lead reports

### Italy: stricter criteria for exceptional-transport derogations for BESS containers (UN 3536) (Italy)

According to an industry report by Ecoblog (published 21 October 2026), the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (MIT) issued a note on 6 July 2026 to the main road transport associations. The note clarifies the procedures for granting the derogations needed to move BESS (Battery Energy Storage System) containers, classified as dangerous goods under UN 3536, as exceptional transport. It introduces more restrictive criteria than those applied until now. The report warns that this could hold up battery storage projects in Italy. This is a trade-press account, not an officially validated legal text.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Italy, national scope. No specific regions or roads are named in the supplied evidence.  
**Affected vehicles:** Exceptional transport (eccezionale) of BESS containers classified as dangerous goods, UN 3536. Applicability to other abnormal loads is not confirmed.

**Impact:** Derogation applications for BESS container moves may face tougher criteria. That can mean longer lead times, refused or conditioned permits and added scrutiny where the ADR dangerous-goods classification overlaps with abnormal-load status. Project schedules for Italian storage sites could be affected.

**Recommended action:** Obtain the MIT note of 6 July 2026 from your association or the permit authority and check it against current BESS applications. Rebuild permit lead times in project plans and confirm the criteria with the competent permit office before booking equipment and escorts. Treat the details in this trade report as unconfirmed until verified against the official text.

**Exemptions/conditions:** No exemptions are stated in the supplied evidence. Do not assume that any general exemption applies to exceptional transport.

### Czechia: government draft would raise HGV toll rates, with air-pollution and CO₂ components rising most (Czechia)

Ekonomický deník (23 October 2026) reports that the government has put a draft amendment to the toll regulation out for comment through the Czech Chamber of Commerce. The infrastructure-cost component would rise by 5.8%, the air-pollution component by 34.8% and the CO₂ emissions component by 70.7%. The noise component would stay unchanged. The state expects about CZK 4 billion in extra revenue and cites alignment with neighbouring countries, especially Austria. Carriers warn that the costs will be passed on to customers. This is a draft at the consultation stage, and the supplied evidence gives no effective date.

**Category:** Operational development  
**Where:** Czechia, national toll system for goods vehicles.  
**Affected vehicles:** Toll-liable goods vehicles. Specific weight or emission classes are not given in the supplied evidence.

**Impact:** If adopted, per-kilometre toll costs on Czech routes would rise, with emission-related components hit hardest, so older and higher-emission vehicles would be most exposed. Czech legs of transit and project-cargo moves would cost more, which matters for quotes and contract indexation.

**Recommended action:** Model the proposed percentage increases against your Czech toll mileage by emission class. Check whether contracts allow toll surcharges to be passed on. Track the consultation outcome and the final regulation text and effective date before changing tariffs.

**Exemptions/conditions:** None stated in the supplied evidence.

*Source: [Ekonomický deník](https://ekonomickydenik.cz/zvyseni-myta-dopravci-zakaznici/) — Industry report — not an official authority notice*
