**Schedule update — 27 October 2026:** Pre-production testing is now planned for Q3 2027 (July–September). The exact start date will be confirmed. Registration remains open.

**Czech Republic, 13 October 2026.** DAJC is inviting European heavy and oversized transport professionals to register for pre-production testing of the DAJC Platform, now planned for **Q3 2027 (July–September)**.

Registration is already open to transport companies, owner-drivers, dispatchers, industrial shippers, escort operators, permit specialists, crane providers and other specialists who work on complex movements across national borders.

[Register for DAJC Platform testing](/testing/).

## Why coordination matters

A single heavy or abnormal transport can depend on route assessments, permits, equipment, authorised participants, documents, infrastructure restrictions and changes during execution. Today, these details are often managed in separate systems and communication threads.

DAJC is being developed as a **provider-neutral coordination and operational-intelligence layer**. Its proposed D-ID transport context is intended to connect the relevant workflow while allowing existing transport-management, telematics and business systems to remain in place.

The development direction comes from founder Miroslav Dajč's first-hand experience in road transport, including heavy and oversized operations. DAJC is not a freight broker or marketplace.

## What testing means

Pre-production participants will help validate the same DAJC Platform product intended for eventual production use, while it remains under active development. Functionality, coverage and readiness will be assessed as work continues. Registration or testing participation does **not** mean a commercial service is already available.

The current public testing invitation and eligibility details are available at [dajc.eu/testing](/testing/).

## A related research direction: DAJC Assistance

DAJC is also researching **DAJC Assistance**: a future capability for more consistent handoffs between a motorist or fleet, the responsible assistance programme or insurer, and authorised roadside service or recovery providers.

The planned scope concerns incidents involving motor vehicles, potentially including both passenger vehicles and heavy commercial transport. The concept is in research and design. DAJC is **not currently operating a roadside-assistance network**, and no geographic coverage, provider partnership, live dispatch integration, price or launch date is being promised.

For actual emergencies, contact the competent emergency services. DAJC Assistance is not a replacement for emergency numbers or public rescue dispatch.

[Read about DAJC Assistance](/assistance/).
