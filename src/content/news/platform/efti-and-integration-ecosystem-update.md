DAJC continues to move forward in two important areas of platform development.

## Preparing for eFTI certification

DAJC is preparing the platform for the future eFTI certification framework in Europe.

The goal is to ensure that DAJC can meet the applicable requirements when the certification process is fully available and relevant to the platform.

This work is preparatory. DAJC is **not currently presenting itself as an eFTI-certified platform**.

## Continuing integration discussions

At the same time, DAJC is continuing discussions with additional organisations and service providers that may become relevant to the wider DAJC ecosystem.

These conversations cover different parts of European heavy and oversized transport and are intended to gradually broaden the range of services, data and infrastructure that can work alongside DAJC.

Only information that is appropriate for public disclosure is published. Current publicly listed integration relationships and their general status can be followed in the [DAJC Integration Ecosystem](/partners/).

## Building carefully, step by step

DAJC is being developed progressively around real transport needs. Public updates will continue to focus on meaningful progress without publishing internal technical details, confidential discussions or development information that is not intended for public use.

More updates will follow as individual developments become suitable for public release.
