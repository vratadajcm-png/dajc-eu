**Schedule update — 27 October 2026:** DAJC Platform pre-production testing is now planned for **Q3 2027 (July–September)**, not January 2027. The exact start date will be confirmed later.

As DAJC Platform progresses towards pre-production testing in Q3 2027, we are exploring three ways to collaborate with the European heavy and oversized transport community **separately from the Platform and its future integration ecosystem**.

## DAJC Media Partnerships — sponsored editorial

Relevant manufacturers, technology providers and specialist businesses can express interest in **clearly identified sponsored articles** on DAJC.eu. Every paid contribution would be visibly labelled, fact-checked and subject to editorial review. Payment would not influence technical assessments, provider rankings or integration decisions.

## DAJC Intelligence Report — preliminary transport review

We are exploring a case-by-case expert review of **cargo and vehicle dimensions, supplied documents and operational risks** before a complex transport is arranged. The output would identify missing information, potential constraints and questions needing confirmation with competent authorities or qualified specialists. It would **not** replace transport permits, an approved route, engineering certification or a legal opinion.

## DAJC Intelligence Pro — planned, not yet available

A tailored information service for carriers and planners is a longer-term proposal. **Intelligence Pro will not be offered until the underlying news workflow reliably generates, verifies, edits and publishes updates and distributes approved summaries to X and LinkedIn.** No subscription or delivery date is being announced now.

**Interested in discussing one of these options?** Write to [team@dajc.eu](mailto:team@dajc.eu?subject=DAJC%20Media%20or%20Intelligence%20enquiry) and describe your organisation and needs.

These are invitations to discuss possible cooperation, **not current offers to order or pay for a live service**. Any commercial engagement depends on the necessary legal and operational arrangements. DAJC Platform and DAJC Assistance remain separate development initiatives, and no public customer functions are being launched by this announcement.
