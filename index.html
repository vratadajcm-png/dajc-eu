DAJC

EUROPEAN HEAVY & OVERSIZE TRANSPORT PLATFORM

DAJC is a pan-European platform for managing heavy and oversized transport. It connects all participants, documents, permits, routes and shipment progress in one digital environment. Using AI and specialized applications, it simplifies planning, communication and transport execution without commissions.
